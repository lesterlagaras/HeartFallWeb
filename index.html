🔥 HeartFall SMP Experience

HeartFall SMP is a vanilla competitive survival server where players are free to use any PvP style mechanics such as crystal PvP, spear, mace, sword, and cart combat. Choose the playstyle you are most comfortable with and dominate your enemies through skill and strategy.

Base griefing and team betrayal are allowed, making alliances risky and gameplay unpredictable. Massive farms are also encouraged as a way to generate income and progress.

The server features a balanced economy system with a shop that includes blocks, tools, food, enchantments, potions, and heart recipes.

Additional features include lifesteal mechanics, competitive PvP environment, events, and a progression system designed for long-term survival gameplay.
